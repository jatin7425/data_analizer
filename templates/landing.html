How it works

  1. Upload a workbook

    The app profiles each column: its type, empty cells, and distinct values.

  2. Review the design

    Check the proposed tables, keys, SQL, and report ideas. Nothing is created until you approve.

  3. Connect PostgreSQL

    Enter a server and database name. Missing tables and columns are added; existing data is left alone.

  4. Import new sheets

    Each upload is checked against the original columns, then rows are inserted or updated without duplicates.
